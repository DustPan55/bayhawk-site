BayHawk

Inshore fishing, predicted.

BayHawk learns from your catches to predict the inshore bite. Every fish and every slow day you log trains a private model on your water, so its predictions get sharper the more you fish. You are the hawk. BayHawk gives you the eyes.

What it does

Built for inshore saltwater anglers chasing redfish, speckled trout, and flounder across the bays, flats, and marshes.

  • A live bite score for every spot, from real conditions: tide, wind, water temp, salinity, pressure, solunar, and bottom structure.
  • See exactly why a spot scored what it did, factor by factor.
  • AI trip plans and pattern insights built from your own spots and catches.
  • Maps with satellite imagery, seagrass, oyster reefs, and depth contours.
  • Catch logging with photo fish and lure ID, bag-limit tracking, and state regulations.
  • An Apple Watch app, offline support, and shareable safety plans.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

BayHawk is built on a simple promise: your fishing data is yours. We do not run ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your spots or your catches.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email you choose to share, used only to create and sync your account.
  • Your fishing data. The spots, catches, notes, and settings you enter in the app.
  • Location. Your device location, used to score nearby spots and center the map. Location is used while you use the app and is not tracked in the background.
  • Photos you choose. If you use fish or lure identification, the photo you select is sent to our processing service to return an identification. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

How we use it

Your data is used only to provide the app's features: predicting the bite, logging catches, identifying fish and lures, and syncing your information across your iPhone, Apple Watch, and the web. Sharing happens only with people you explicitly invite.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on our backend provider so it can sync between your devices. It is protected in transit and at rest, and is associated with your account.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking that identifies you across other apps or sites.
  • No selling or renting of your personal data.

Your control

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app, under Settings, Account, Delete account. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

BayHawk is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
